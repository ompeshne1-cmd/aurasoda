The Heritage Philosophy

Why Settle For Chemical Sodas When You Can Drink Art?

In the 1880s, soda brewing was an apothecary’s culinary devotion—herbal tinctures steeped over low flames, rare spices ground by hand, and whole fruits squeezed at peak harvest.

At Heritage Brew, we rejected the modern shortcut of neon artificial dyes and corn syrup syrups. We slow-brew in small copper kettles, marry our elixirs with charred French oak staves, and seal every bottle under velvety champagne effervescence.
