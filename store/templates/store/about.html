The Brewery Chronology

A Passion for Uncompromising Craft

How we turned back the clock to rescue the lost apothecary tradition of hand-crafted sparkling sodas.

Charred Cask Aging

We steep our base elixirs with toasted French and American oak wood, delivering complex vanilla, toffee, and toasted campfire undertones rarely found outside barrel-aged spirits.

Whole Culinary Fruit

No artificial flavoring 'essences'. We cold-press whole Sicilian blood oranges, simmer organic ginger roots, and steam fresh lemongrass stalks directly in our kettles.

Champagne Carbonation

Mass-market sodas use harsh, sharp carbonation to mask flat synthetic flavors. Our slow-dissolved micro-effervescence delivers silky, persistent bubbles that dance lightly on the palate.

A Note from the Founder

"Real Soda Deserves A Seat At The Fine Dining Table."

"For decades, consumers had two choices: neon chemical sodas laden with high-fructose corn syrup, or bland sparkling waters. We founded Heritage Brew to offer something profound: an artisanal soft drink crafted with the reverence of fine wine or craft beer, yet entirely non-alcoholic and pure."

Arthur Sterling Master Cellar Brewer, Heritage Brew Co.
